# Board design notes

How the board pages under `/p/` are built, so new pages read the same. The marketing page set the register; the board follows it.

> Copied from the site's hosted/DESIGN.md (site bff92c0, 4 Nov 2026), when the local board moved into the CLI. These are the local board's design notes now: they can change here without changing the team board's, and paths like `components/ui/type.tsx` mean `board/components/ui/type.tsx`.

## Type hierarchy

Hierarchy comes from **colour and size, not weight**. Three weights: regular for values, medium for one thing only — a decision's Change line — and semibold for titles. Nothing is uppercase; nothing is letter-spaced. Use the primitives in `components/ui/type.tsx` rather than sizes by hand.

| Role | Primitive | Looks like |
|---|---|---|
| The page's one subject | `PageTitle` | 22px semibold, tight tracking, black |
| One sentence under it | `Lede` | 15px regular, grey |
| A section on the page | `SectionTitle` | 15px semibold tight; counts after it in regular grey (`count`), the least important bit fainter (`aside`) |
| The left column of a key/value | `Label` | 13px regular grey |
| The value | plain text | 14px regular black; a secondary value (a rejected option, a pattern) is grey |
| Who · when · where | `Meta` | 13px grey |
| An id — `D-0017`, `C-0004` | `Id` | 12px mono grey |
| The least important thing on a line | `Faint` | 12px faint |
| Category, status, verdict | `Badge` | Title case, always: `Interaction`, `Rule`, `Rule Clash` |

Layouts: `KeyValues` (fixed label column, values beside) for decisions and property blocks; `PropertyRow` + `Property` for the inline row under a page title.

## Structure of a screen page

Breadcrumb → `PageTitle` → `Lede` (the reason, in quotes) → `PropertyRow` (Status · Area · Decided by · Date · Live) → the screenshot in its own tinted card → "Other options" chips → **Decided here** → **Comments** → footer.

**Versions.** A piece of work keeps its earlier pictures (the last ten, in `.arbiter/work/history/`). When it has had more than one, a row under the picture shows a small thumbnail for each, oldest first, the one showing outlined, named on hover with its number and when it was taken ("Version 2 · 3 days ago"); then "3 of 3" and Compare. Compare opens a before and after: two pictures one over the other, wiped by dragging across or with the slider under it, with Before and After picked at the top. Decided here then groups its decisions under the picture each led to, newest first: a heading per version, "Version 2" in semibold, when it was taken in muted, and "Show version" faint at the far right, the same on every version, in line with the decisions' counts. A hairline sits under each heading and none between the decisions under it, since the version already groups them (an exception to D-0017's divided list, D-0034). Every version gets its heading, with "No new decisions at this version." when it has none; decisions since the last picture come first, under "Since the last picture". Decisions count by date. No timeline rail. A change with one picture looks as it always did. In the Review inbox, a decision whose change was pictured again after it was queued shows Before beside Now, with Compare.

On the live board a "⋯" sits at the right of the title, the toolbar's height, as on Design rules, each item with an icon: Rename (a piece of work only), Merge into… (a piece of work, when there's another: for work started under a new name that continues an existing change; pick the change, and its decisions, pictures and comments join it), Review what's waiting (with its count, when something is), Copy name, and Copy the picture command (when it has no picture). Rename opens a dialog with two fields, Area (one of the areas there, or a new one) and Title, and shows the name it will save as, "Area — Title", so nobody types the dash. Its decisions, picture, waiting items and comments move with it, the agent hears the new name, and the page moves to its new address. A name another change already has is refused, with why, in red.

On the live board each decision has the actions a rule has on Design rules, faint on hover or focus under its values: Copy (the whole decision as it reads, id first), Edit, Make it a rule (a decision only confirmed) and Remove. On a rule in force, Edit and Remove are Design rules' own (new words for the rule; retired, with why); on any other decision, Edit changes its Change line and Remove takes it out of the record. Old words stay on the record. A removal stays in place, faded, with Undo while the page is open; removing a change's only decision takes the change off the board and back to the Overview. Making it a rule checks the rules there; on an overlap a dialog shows this one first, then the rules it overlaps together in one box under a Your rule label, each with why ("Both apply to plan cards"; a file by its change's name, "Both apply to Old review page"; or "Similar words: …" for a guess) and its own Replace, then Cancel or Keep both. When DECISIONS.md is full, a second dialog of the same shape lists every rule there, each with Replace, then Cancel or Raise the limit (to Preferences). In the Review inbox, Make it a rule asks with the same dialogs (`RuleDialogs.tsx`), plus one more when it clashes with the code, titled This clashes with the code: each thing the files say otherwise as a plain sentence, then Cancel or Make it a rule anyway. On the inbox's own cards, a decision that goes against a rule carries a flagged Rule Clash pill on its top line, and one whose files say otherwise a flagged Code Clash pill, each named on hover; a decision can carry both. The files are read again each time the inbox loads, so a fix made since clears the Code Clash and code broken since shows one. The card says what it clashes with in one row, last, just above the buttons: a red Clashes label (the flag is the pills' alone), then, in the rows' own muted text, the rule ("D-0013 An empty list is one grey line"), its id in the chip the code has, linking to it under Design rules with its reason on hover, and each thing the code says otherwise, one sentence a line, the same words as the dialog: the file by its name, then what's in it now ("billing.css is missing var(--brand)", "list.css still has text-overflow", "plans.css isn’t in the project any more"), so what's wrong is the second word you read. The file and the code each sit in a small chip in the board's mono (the file's whole path on hover), a hex colour with a swatch of itself in front; the words between stay muted. No line numbers, and no "The agent said": the Clashes label and the pill already say the files disagree with the agent. A rule clash's buttons are Keep as exception and Follow D-0013, both outlined (none filled: you say which wins), then Make this the rule (it replaces D-0013) and Skip as text, in the places Confirm, Make it a rule and Skip hold on other cards. In Single, each button's key is its number, 1 for the first, shown on the button; the old letters (C, R, S, F, or E, F, R, S on a clash) still work. Every card button says what it does on hover ("Replaces D-0013, which moves to the archive."). A clash, either kind, is never in Confirm all or Skip all, for the inbox or a group: you check each one, and the Confirm all dialog says how many stay. "Clash" is the one word for both, never "unverified" or "goes against". Remove confirms in a dialog too, a rule asking why. A rule since retired reads Retired.

A decision is: `Id` + dimension `Badge` + verdict `Badge` (Rule; once it's out of DECISIONS.md, Replaced when a newer rule took its place, else Retired, either one saying how it left on hover, "Retired today: Trying new empty states for projects", and linking to Design rules; none for one confirmed or fixed, since a fix already shows in its Rejected row as the agent's words "corrected by" you), "n of N" faint at the right, then `KeyValues` in this order — Change (medium), Rule or Pattern, Rejected, Applies to. Same order and words as the widget in Claude Code. An exception to a rule says so the way a clash does in the Review inbox: a yellow Exception pill on its top line, and an Exception to row last, the rule's id in the chip the clashes use (linking to it under Design rules, its reason on hover), then its words in muted. The label stays grey, not red, since the call is made. It has no Make it a rule (as a rule it would say the opposite of the one it's an exception to), and Copy adds the same line. Decisions sit about 54px apart, the same in a divided list and in a version section: 20px above each, and below it its faint Copy and Edit row and 8px (32px in an exported copy, which has no such row). Comments sits as far below the last decision, its section 22px under it on the live board instead of the usual 40px, since that faint row is already there. Discussion on a decision is indented under it with a rail and a comment glyph. The ways to add to it ("Comment on this", or "Reply" once there's a thread, and "Request change") show only on hover or keyboard focus, thread or no thread: the discussion stays in view, the actions don't. One comment sits inline; two or more fold to one row — chevron, count, who spoke last, when — until opened, so a screen with talk on every decision still reads as a list of decisions.

Lists of like things are divided by hairlines, not boxed in cards. Cards are for things that are clickable as a whole (a screen on the board).

## Words

The board speaks design, not Arbiter. It's a set of **changes**, not screens — a font swap is a change with no screen. The record keeps its own words (`generated`, `superseded`, `recorded`, `rule`, piece of work); only the board translates them:

| Record | Board |
|---|---|
| All screens | **Overview** · "12 changes" |
| Section | **Area** |
| Directions for a feature | **Options** |
| `generated`, `in_review` · `approved` · `rejected`, `superseded` · `recorded` | **Proposed** · **Approved** · **Rejected** · **Approved** (Proposed for an option not chosen between yet; one word for signed off, work or option; Rejected is DECISIONS.md's word for an alternative that lost. **Decided here** and **Decided by** on a page stay: they're about the decisions) |
| Rule · Standing rules | **Rule** · **Design rules** (how designers say it; was Guideline · Guidelines) |

## Plain changes and areas

Most decisions come from one direction, iterated, not options compared. They still publish: one card per piece of work (`W-…`, Approved once everything on it is judged), named for what was being built. Its page has no "Other options" — title, properties, the screenshot if one was taken, Decided here, Comments.

Every card sits in an **area** (`areasFor` in `lib/board.ts`). A set of options has the feature it was explored for. A plain change takes its area from its name — "Settings — billing" is Settings; "Reports filters" is Reports when Reports is an area on the board. A change where every decision applies everywhere is **Foundations** (type, colour, spacing, voice). Anything else is an area of its own, by its name. There's no catch-all.

## The other pages

**Design rules** is the same list shape, grouped by dimension, under a subline that says where they live: "8 rules you've saved in DECISIONS.md. Everything new follows them." Under it, the Overview's filter bar: Type (all seven types, each with how many rules it has, 0 included) and search over a rule, its reason and its id, both kept in the address, and Clear filters (the Overview's words too) to put them back. `Id` in the label column, the rule beside it in fg, its reason under in muted. No cards — nothing here is clickable as a whole. A rule decided on a screen of this board says so under its reason (`Meta`: "Decided on …"), and each row is an anchor: the `rule` badge on a screen page points at it, and the id goes fg when it's the target. Under that, a rule with exceptions has an asterisk (a footnote to the rule) and how many, in amber on a grey pill, and one with clashes waiting the Review inbox's tray in blue with how many: the grey count pills a card has, decided in green beside waiting in blue. Their words are on hover, "1 exception". Each opens in place to list its own, one a line: the pill a change's page gives it (the yellow Exception pill keeps its word there), the change's name without its area, and its Change line. An exception links to its decision on its change's page, a clash to its work in the inbox. An exported copy shows the exceptions, never what's waiting. At the bottom sits **Retired**, with how many, closed until you open it (a filter that finds one there, or a link to one, opens it): each rule taken out of DECISIONS.md, the latest to go first, its words and reason quieter than a rule in force, then how it left under a label in the Review inbox's Clashes red (13px): Reason and why it was retired, or Replaced by and the rule that took its place, its id a chip linking to the rule in force. The day it left sits where a rule in force has its date. No pill: the section says Retired, and the label says which way it went. A line under the title says your agent no longer follows them. Copy on a rule gives what Copy on its change's page gives: the whole decision, id first.

**Overview** shows each change as a card (clickable as a whole) or, in list view, as a bordered row. On a card: the area in `Faint` above the name, only when the card isn't under its area's heading; the name semibold tight, up to two lines, without the area; then one description in regular muted, up to two lines, the first the card's width and the last stopping about two words short of the edge, the same one a row shows: the reason in quotes (never italic), else what changed first; then, under it, one line: a pill and the counts as icons and numbers. Besides a set's options pill, the only pill a card has is what's waiting for you, as the Review inbox's tray and a count in the Waiting colour. Approved, the usual case, has none, for work and for a set alike: a board of Approved pills hides the few that matter. **A set of options is one card**, so the work stays the main thing on the Overview: a set is the options in one area whose names start the same ("Empty state — illustration", "Empty state — checklist"), versions of one thing, and the card stands for them all. It's named for what they share ("Empty state", with its area, Projects, beside it like any card), or by its area when the names only say that ("Project card"). Two things explored in one area stay two sets, and approving an option replaces only the rest of its own set. It shows the approved option, else the first still open, and links to that option's page, where Other options lists the rest. Its description is one line: "Compact approved" once you've chosen, "Illustration, one line, checklist" while none is chosen (each named without what they all start with, so the list says how they differ), or "None approved" when every one is rejected (that card fades). How many options it has is a grey pill, last of the pills in its line (after what's waiting and the decisions): diverging arrows and the count, "3 options" on hover. Once you've approved one it counts the others, since the line already names it ("3 other options"). Grey, since it asks nothing of you. What's waiting on any of its options adds up on it. A rejected option never has a card of its own; it's on its set's pages, with its pill, and one rejected because another was approved says so beside the pill ("Card — compact was approved instead"). The side nav and the area filter count a set as one. The Status filter has three, each with its pill's colour as a dot: Waiting, Proposed (a set with none chosen yet) and Approved. An option's page has no buttons: you choose by telling your agent, which records the reason in your words. Under the title, one line: the reason in quotes, then a faint dot and the agent's few words on what the option is (“Reads fastest in a long list” · Name and last edit only). In the Status row its own pill comes first, then Waiting beside its count ("Approved · Waiting · 1 to review"). One not chosen yet is **Proposed**, a grey pill (it isn't waiting on you in the inbox, so not blue), with "Not chosen yet. Tell your agent which one to keep." on hover, and has no Decided by. Under Decided here it has a banner the shape of Waiting's, about the choice: "3 options, none chosen yet" and "Tell your agent which one to keep, and why. The other options become Rejected, with your reason." It has no button, since choosing happens with the agent. One added after you approved another says that one is approved instead. Other options shows each one's pill, Proposed included. The agent queues an option's decisions only once you've chosen it, so nothing waits on a version that may not stay. The decisions count is the same kind of pill, a tick in a box and a count, in `ok` green: to do in blue, done in green, side by side. Comments and open requests are plain icons and numbers with the thread's own glyphs. Each is named on hover and to a screen reader, and whatever a change hasn't got takes no room (D-0032). At the far right of that line, in `Faint`, when it last changed: its latest decision, judged or waiting, as Today, Yesterday, 3 days ago, then a date; the exact time on hover. A row (list view) is the name with one line of what changed under it; on the right, the pill and the counts side by side, and under them in `Faint` the area and when it last changed. No author and no time of day: on the local board it's always you. On a phone the right-hand side moves under the description. Empty states and notices use a semibold title and a muted sentence, nothing heavier.

**Preferences** (live board only, last in the rail with a sliders icon, on its own) uses the Design rules shape: a title, a subline saying the settings live in arbiter.json, then groups under section titles, each with a line on what it covers. A hairline sits above each group, the first included; the settings inside a group aren't ruled. A row is the setting's name on the left (14px, fg) and its control on the right, with what it does under it in muted. A choice of a few is a segmented control, the toolbar's height, the chosen one tinted; on or off is a switch with its words beside it. Each saves as you change it and "Saved" shows briefly at the top right; a refused value says why in red under its field and goes back. The review question isn't a preference: it comes at every 5 waiting (D-0005), so its row's words say so instead. An exported copy has no Preferences.

The left rail carries hierarchy by colour alone: group labels faint, items muted, the current one fg on a tint with a rail mark. No weight change on selection. On the live board Design rules is always in it, with no rules too (right after the last one is removed, its Undo is on that page); an exported copy lists it only when it has rules.

**Boxes and errors.** Every box that saves works the same (`keys.ts`): Enter saves, Shift+Enter adds a line, Escape cancels, and Enter while an input method is still putting a word together picks the word instead of saving. An error shows in red under what it's about, starts with a capital, and is read out when it appears; typing in the box again or putting the old value back clears it. A dialog that's saving can't be closed until it's done.

**Folders** sit under Areas: you make them ("⋯" on the Areas heading: New folder, Sort A to Z), name them in place, and drag areas into them or rows up and down. A folder holds areas by name, one level deep, with no folder inside a folder; its row has a chevron to open it and its total count. Each row's "⋯" (on hover or focus) does what a drag does, for the keyboard: Move to, Move up, Move down, and on a folder Rename and Remove folder, which puts its areas back. A drag shows where it lands as a line above or below a row, or a tint on the folder it goes into. Clicking a folder shows its areas on the Overview, each under its own heading. They're saved in `.arbiter/areas.json`, so the project carries them; an exported copy shows them and nothing more.

## Menus and icon buttons

Every "⋯" menu reads the same: each item has a 14px icon left of its words, from one set (`components/ui/icons.tsx`), muted until the item is highlighted, and a count on the right when there is one. A page's own "⋯" (Design rules, the Review inbox's Confirm or skip all, a change's page) is the bar's height with its outline (`barDots`) and names itself on hover; a row's "⋯" (an inbox group, the side nav) is 28px or smaller and quiet until hovered. Everything that says something on hover (an icon-only button's name, a count pill's words, a date's exact time, a full name cut short) uses the board's black tooltip (`components/ui/tooltip.tsx`), never the browser's `title`. It sits above dialogs, so it shows in the picture viewer too. Empty states are one size: a semibold title and a muted sentence, in a dashed box `py-16` when the page has nothing at all, without the box when a filter left nothing.

## Dates

One way everywhere, through `components/When.tsx`: Today, Yesterday, 3 days ago, then the date as "4 Nov" (with the year only when it isn't this one). A change's own page adds the time of day ("2 Nov, 5:39 pm"); lists show the day alone. Times are in the viewer's own clock, the same as "Updated" in the top bar. The whole date and time show on hover. "As of" a date shows only on a read-only copy, as "as of 11 Nov 2026"; the local board is always as of now.

## Surfaces and colour

`bg` for the page, `surface` for cards and the rail, `surface-2` for tinted panels (the screenshot frame, hover states). Colour only when it carries meaning: `ok` for Approved and rules; `warn` for Rejected and an exception's pill (and the team board's In review); grey for Proposed, like Retired. Amber (`warn`) is for pills only: text that isn't a pill is never amber, so a subline near a limit or a notice like "Taken back…" stays in the page's own text colours. Dark mode inverts through the same tokens; never hard-code a colour.

## Names and casing

Names are capitalised for display even when the record has them lowercase. Ids stay mono. Section names are shown as recorded.
